# G2 cut ledger: spawn, harness adapters, providers, hooks and the rest of fno-agents

Base `aa9a1f7e4d` (origin/main at start). Scope: the 576 files the path rule in the [README](README.md#campaigns) gives G2. That is every `crates/fno-agents` path matching no G3 or G1 Rust word, plus `crates/fno-agents/tests/done_close_note.rs`, which landed after the map was cut.

The cut stops short of the target. This ledger names each kept contract per the README's stop-short clause. The roughly 450 files this pass did not reach are the campaign's continuation, which owns the remaining 1,230-case cut.

The map's per-file before counts predate my base by nine declarations of drift on five files and one post-map file. The rows below carry the map values.

## Method

The keep rule is the README's: one contract, one keeper, deletion owes no per-test evidence. The cut classes, with named examples:

- Refusal families fold to one case per fail-closed guard. `hook/test_run_guard.rs` 14->6. `resume_pin.rs` 13->6. `verify_evidence.rs` 13->0. `kill_criteria.rs` 4->0. `pane_stop.rs` 12->8. In each, the family's keeper names its remedies or both reads.
- Value tables cut to the happy, refusal and one edge. `codex_ask_unit.rs` 41->27. `agents_config.rs` 32->29. `manifest.rs` 27->21. The exit-code map keeps pass-through, provider-unavailable and interrupted. The sandbox flag units fold into the composed argv keepers.
- Units beneath a real-command or parity boundary die. `verify_evidence.rs` and `kill_criteria.rs` went to zero under their parity files. `skill_diff.rs` 12->7, where the parity goldens cover the parse, rank and propose boundary. `claude_ask.rs` 13->8, whose envelope, seed, socket and followup tables fold under `tests/claude_ask_dispatch.rs`.
- Same-branch repeats die. Examples: the SIG-stop repeats in `test_run_lifecycle.rs` and `codex_ask_unit.rs`. Also dead: the parser branch repeats in `loops_pause_quiet.rs`. `spawn_routing.rs` keeps one unknown-name refusal per door plus the stderr byte pin.

Every edit ran through a brace-matched cutter. A wrong name match or an unexpected count change refused the write. The tool refused twice, and both files re-ran clean. The dead helpers and imports the fold left (fifteen across nine files) were removed. One was a `#[cfg(test)]` re-export item in `loopcheck.rs`, a G3 file, removed as cut-leftover cleanup per the G1 lead precedent. The compile gate still peeled more stragglers in CI after the ledger landed. Each was definition-only: claude_ask's `tmpdir`, `write_session`, `write_roster` and the `ENV_LOCK` static, one unused fake-daemon builder, and two unused loop_runtime helpers. The follow-up commits swept them by static call-site scan while the machine brake blocked a local compile.

Preservation: every cut is a fold of a family whose keeper stays, or a unit under a stronger boundary that stays. CI exercises the keepers. The rulebook's preservation spot-check mutates five kept contracts and expects the keeper to go red. It was not run for this ledger. The deviation is recorded here by the review round. The keepers' verification instead rides CI green plus this round's re-derived boundary checks. The kill-criteria parity keeper stays at fourteen tests. The done_probes fail-closed contracts moved under tests/loop_check.rs, which covers them through the real settings merge.
