# G2 cut ledger: spawn, harness adapters, providers, hooks and the rest of fno-agents

Base `aa9a1f7e4d` (origin/main at start). Scope: the 576 files the path rule in the [README](README.md#campaigns) gives G2 - every `crates/fno-agents` path that matches no G3 or G1 Rust word, plus `crates/fno-agents/tests/done_close_note.rs`, which landed after the map was cut.

Before: 2,761 declarations and, because Rust collects one case per declaration, 2,761 collected cases. After: 2,610. The cut is 151 cases, 5.5 percent of the scope. The campaign target is 1,380 (half of the scope's collected cases). The cut stops short of the target: this ledger names each kept contract per the README's stop-short clause, and the roughly 450 files this pass did not reach are the campaign's continuation, which owns the remaining 1,230-case cut.

The map's per-file before counts predate my base by nine declarations of drift on five files and one post-map file; the rows below carry the map values.

## Method

The keep rule is the README's: one contract, one keeper, deletion owes no per-test evidence. The cut classes, with named examples:

- Refusal families fold to one case per fail-closed guard. Examples: `hook/test_run_guard.rs` 14->6 (the whole-suite refusal keeps one case; the allow edges keep the compound and heredoc false-positive guards); `resume_pin.rs` 13->6 (the route resolution decision table keeps the happy path, the birth pin, and the refusal that names both reads); `verify_evidence.rs` 13->0 (src units beneath the parity characterization keeper; the parity file is the boundary), `kill_criteria.rs` 4->0 (same class). `pane_stop.rs` 12->8 (the never-signal-an-unproven-pid guard keeps `ac1_edge_unverified_pid_never_kills_or_signals` plus the lsof branch sweep and the real-lsof integration).
- Value tables cut to the happy, refusal and one edge. Examples: `codex_ask_unit.rs` 41->27 (the exit-code map keeps pass-through, provider-unavailable and interrupted; the sandbox flag units fold into the composed argv keepers); `agents_config.rs` 32->29; `manifest.rs` 27->21 (the parse-guard refusals keep the fail-closed composite-gate keeper).
- Units beneath a real-command or parity boundary die. Named: `verify_evidence.rs` and `kill_criteria.rs` to zero under their parity files; `skill_diff.rs` 12->7 (the parity goldens cover the parse/rank/propose boundary; the tick and reconcile contracts stay); `claude_ask.rs` 13->8 (the envelope, seed, socket and followup tables fold under `tests/claude_ask_dispatch.rs`).
- Same-branch repeats die. Examples: the two SIG-stop repeats in `test_run_lifecycle.rs`; the parser branch repeats in `codex_ask_unit.rs` and `loops_pause_quiet.rs`; `spawn_routing.rs` keeps one unknown-name refusal case per door plus the stderr byte pin.

Every edit ran through a brace-matched cutter with a fail-closed refusal when a name matched wrong or a count changed unexpectedly; the tool refused twice and both files were re-run clean. Compile check `cargo check --all-targets` ran after the cut and is clean at zero warnings; the dead helpers and imports the fold left (thirteen across nine files) were removed, including one `#[cfg(test)]` re-export item in `loopcheck.rs`, a G3 file, as cut-leftover cleanup per the G1 lead precedent.

Preservation: every cut is a fold of a family whose keeper stays, or a unit under a stronger boundary that stays. CI exercises the keepers; the campaign's preservation review rides CI and the review lane.
