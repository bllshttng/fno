Usage: fno backlog session [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  Append-only lifecycle session provenance ().

Commands:
  add        Stamp a node with a lifecycle phase record (idempotent, append-only).
             --origin fills started_at from the named session's transcript and
             first-fills the node's origin session (never overwrites).
  open       Hold node:<id> under blueprint-session:<id> for this session's planner.
  close      Close the blueprint phase with one identity-guarded completion receipt.
  backfill   Fill missing session starts and ends from transcripts and merge commits.
  reap-open  Reap one exact open session row after the observer proves session death.
