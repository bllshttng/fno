Usage: fno backlog batch <verb> [options]

Batch-lane state: coalesce same-domain nodes into one PR (opt-in).

Verbs:
  open --domain <d> --branch <b> --worktree <w> [--max-nodes <n>]
  join --domain <d> --node <id> [--summary <s>]
  ship --domain <d> [--base <b>] [--title <t>]
  prepare --node <id> --repo <main-checkout> [--root <r>]
  ship-closeable [--project <p>] [--mission <m>]
  status [--domain <d>]
  metrics [--since <ts>] [--json]

State: <canonical>/.fno/batches/<domain>.json under one flock per domain.
Exit codes: open 3 exists / 1 invalid; join 2 no-batch / 4 full / 1 invalid;
ship 2 abandoned / 3 noop / 0 shipped; prepare always 0 (fail-safe solo).
`batch close` and `batch abandon` are retired: `ship` is the close path
(it requeues members as individual PRs on failure).
